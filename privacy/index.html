Privacy policy

Nueve is a sudoku by dothecode. We don't collect personal or usage data.

  • No accounts, no ads, no tracking. The app has no sign-up, advertising, analytics or third-party tracking tools.
  • What is saved stays on your phone. Your game in progress, your preferences (visual help and language) and your game history (time, mistakes and hints) are stored only on the device. We don't send them anywhere. Deleting the app deletes them.
